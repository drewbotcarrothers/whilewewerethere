After taking a high-speed train from Florence to Naples and changing onto the Circumvesuviana train, the cone of Mount Vesuvius appeared across flat green fields outside our window—and by early afternoon we were standing above a steep cliff road in Sorrento looking straight out to the Bay of Naples. After six busy city days in Paris and Florence, everything opened up into limestone cliffs, fishing boats, and salt air.

Michelle and I stayed four nights in Sorrento. Using one town as a home base let us see Sorrento's harbour, spend a beach day in Positano, fit in our [Pompeii day trip](/blog/pompeii-day-trip-from-sorrento/), and take a morning public ferry to Capri—the brightest stretch of our [trip from Paris to Rome](/blog/paris-florence-sorrento-rome-2013/).

## Why base in Sorrento for the Amalfi Coast and Capri?

Sorrento sits on a cliff terrace facing Naples and Vesuvius, with direct boat access to Capri and Positano plus rail access toward Pompeii and Naples. Rather than hauling suitcases along steep Amalfi staircases, we checked into one room in Sorrento for four nights and went out light each morning.

### Day 1: Arriving in Sorrento and Marina Grande

After dropping our bags at <a href="https://www.tripadvisor.ca/Hotel_Review-g187782-d3224156-Reviews-Green_House_Sorrento-Sorrento_Province_of_Naples_Campania.html" rel="nofollow">Green House</a> on Via degli Aranci in central Sorrento, we walked through the narrow lanes of the old town and down to Marina Grande. From the terrace above, you look straight over terracotta roofs and a green-domed church tower into a small crescent harbour full of wooden boats. Walking out along the piers later that afternoon, Vesuvius sat clear across the Bay of Naples to the north.

### Day 2: Positano along the Amalfi Coast road

Morning started early on a SITA bus along the coastal corniche heading toward Positano. Every bend in the road hangs straight above the Tyrrhenian Sea until Positano suddenly piles up the cliff ahead of you, white and pastel houses stacked above the bay.

### Day 3: Pompeii day trip

We took the Circumvesuviana train from Sorrento to Pompeii—very convenient with the station right outside the entrance—and explored on a self-guided tour with a Rick Steves audio guide. That full day has its own guide: [Pompeii Day Trip from Sorrento](/blog/pompeii-day-trip-from-sorrento/).

### Day 4: Capri by boat and the high Positano overlook

We were down at Sorrento's Marina Piccola port early on Day 4 to catch the public ferry to Capri before the water turned busy—the boat ride along the coast was breathtaking. By mid-morning the white limestone cliffs of Capri were towering over the water, and we passed sea caves and the Faraglioni—three sea stacks rising straight out of deep blue water, one pierced clean through by a natural arch.

## What was worth it—and what would we skip or change?

Taking an early public ferry out to Capri from Marina Piccola was the standout morning of the coast. Seeing the limestone cliffs and the Faraglioni from the water before noon beats spending half your day waiting in funicular and bus queues up in Capri town.

What we would change is doubling back along the same busy coastal road more than needed. The views from the corniche are dramatic, but traffic can crawl in good weather; pairing one road leg with one ferry leg cuts down the time spent watching brake lights.

## How do you get around Sorrento, Positano, and Capri?

Sorrento has reliable seasonal ferry links to both Capri and Positano from Marina Piccola, while [SITA Sud](https://sitasudtrasporti.it/campania/orari/5070-amalfi-positano-sorrento-via-meta-via-massa-lubrense-via-nastro-verde/) buses (Route 5070) run along the Amalfi Drive (SS163) between Sorrento, Positano, and Amalfi—board outside Sorrento's Circumvesuviana station, buy tickets at an authorized tabacchi or newsstand before boarding, and validate them on board (the 24-hour [COSTIERASITA](https://sitasudtrasporti.it/campania/avvisi/titolo-di-viaggio-costierasita/) pass covers unlimited SITA Sud rides along the coast). Between Sorrento, Pompeii, and Naples, EAV's Circumvesuviana commuter rail is the rail link along the bay (board underneath Napoli Centrale at Napoli Piazza Garibaldi, and verify the departure board shows Sorrento).

Because ferry timetables, bus crowding rules, and beach club prices change every season, always check current schedules and prices on official local operators before you lock in a day plan.

## What should families know before visiting with kids?

If you are planning this stretch with kids, Sorrento is still the easiest base over Positano. Sorrento has a flatter town centre, easier stroller sidewalks, and quick access to the port, whereas Positano is essentially one giant staircase between the upper road and the beach.

- **Pick ferries over buses for curvy roads:** Boat crossings from Sorrento to Positano or Capri are shorter, cooler, and far easier on kids who get motion-sick.
- **Pack water shoes:** Spiaggia Grande and Marina Grande have dark pebbles and stones rather than soft sand, and the pebbles get hot by midday.
- **Keep Capri to a half-day boat loop:** Kids enjoy riding past sea caves and spotting the Faraglioni arch from the deck without having to wait in long island shuttle lines.
