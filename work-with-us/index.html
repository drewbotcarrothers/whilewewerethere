Work with us

We are a Toronto family — Andrew, Michelle, Harrison, Sullivan, and Maeve — publishing the trips we take and the days we spend close to home, on this site and on YouTube.

Brands that fit that life are welcome to write. A partnership can be a story on the site, a mention in a video, or a film of its own. Sponsored work will be labeled, on the page and on YouTube. We still only publish days we were actually there, and we only recommend things we have used.

Ways we can work together

  • A labeled story on the site about a place, product, or experience we used
  • A YouTube film, with the sponsor named in the video and in the description
  • A shorter mention inside a story we were already going to tell
  • A digital guide in the shop, if the subject is one we can write honestly

What we will not do

  • Pretend we took a trip we did not take
  • Recommend something we have not used as a family
  • Hide that a story or a video was sponsored
  • Hand over our children's full names or private details
