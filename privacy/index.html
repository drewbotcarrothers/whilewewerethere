Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026. This page describes how this website actually works. It is not legal advice.

Who we are

While We Were There is a family journal run by Andrew and Michelle in Toronto, Canada. The public site uses first names only. You can reach us at contact@whilewewerethere.com.

What we publish

Stories about our own family trips and local days, plus the matching videos on YouTube. We do not publish accounts of trips we did not take.

Information you send us

The contact page does not submit to a server of ours. It opens your email app addressed to contact@whilewewerethere.com. If you email us, we use your message and your address to reply, and we keep the email in our inbox like any other letter. We do not sell that correspondence or add it to an advertising list.

Accounts, analytics, and cookies we set

There are no accounts and no login. At the time of this policy we do not run our own analytics and we do not set advertising cookies. The site is static files on ordinary web hosting.

YouTube

Story pages can embed a video with YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com). If you press play, YouTube may set cookies and receive your IP address under Google's privacy policy. The header, footer, and posts also link to our channel. Following those links is a visit to YouTube.

Ads

The site has reserved spots for advertising. They are switched off, and no ad network is loaded. If we turn ads on, the ad slot will be labeled Advertisement, and the provider we choose may use cookies to measure or personalize ads. We will update this page before that happens.

Affiliate links

We are not running affiliate links yet. When we do, the page will show an affiliate disclosure. An affiliate link means we may earn a commission if you buy, at no extra price to you. Those links go to other sites, which have their own privacy practices. We only plan to recommend things we have used as a family.

Sponsorships

A brand may sponsor a story or a video. Sponsored work will be labeled on the page and, when it is a video, on YouTube. Sponsorship does not give a brand your email address.

Digital guides

The shop lists downloadable guides when we have them. This website does not take card numbers and does not run a checkout. A buy button sends you to a payment page we configure, such as a digital-download service. That service, not this site, processes the payment and delivers the file, under its own privacy policy.

Children

The stories are about our children. The site is written for adults who like family travel. We do not ask children to create accounts, send us information, or buy anything. Please do not email us personal information about a child who is not yours.

Hosting

The site is hosted as static files. The host may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP address and the page requested, to operate and secure the service. We do not use those logs to build marketing profiles.

Changes

If we start using ads, analytics, or a new checkout service, we will update this page and the date at the top.
